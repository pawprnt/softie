# Features

## Water reminders
A toast reminder every `water.interval_min` minutes (default 60). Log a sip with
**I drank water** in the dashboard or **I drank water** in the tray menu. Tracks
today's count and a consecutive-day streak.

## Stretch reminders
A popup with a random stretch routine every `stretch.interval_min` minutes
(default 45). Trigger one immediately from the tray menu (**Stretch now**) or the
dashboard (**Stretch**).

## Affirmations
A gentle affirmation toast every `affirmation.interval_min` minutes (default
120). Add your own in Settings; built-ins are always mixed in.

## Daily self-care checklist
A list of recurring tasks (e.g. *take meds*, *go for a walk*, *journal a line*)
shown in the dashboard with checkboxes. Completion resets each day and is
summarized by the **progress ring** (percentage of tasks done today). Add or
remove tasks inline.

## Settings
Intervals and on/off toggles per reminder, plus your custom affirmations.

## System tray
softie runs primarily from the tray (a generated pastel icon). The dashboard is
optional and opens from the tray menu.

## Breathing pacer
A calming animated circle that expands and contracts to guide your breath
(breathe in → hold → breathe out → hold). Open it from the tray (**Breathe**) or
the dashboard.

## Sound & themes
A soft generated chime plays on reminders (toggle in Settings). Themes support a
dark and a light pastel variant, switchable live from Settings.
